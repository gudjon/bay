# Code of conduct

bay follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, be patient, assume good faith, and keep criticism about the work, not the person.
Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not welcome in any bay space: issues,
pull requests, discussions, or anywhere you speak for the project.

To report a problem, email [hello@getbay.io](mailto:hello@getbay.io). Reports are read by the
maintainers and kept confidential. The maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may
ban people who break this code, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
